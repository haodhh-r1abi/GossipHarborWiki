Truyện Bake-off

bakeout_storyintro

  • Quinn (confused): You hear that?
  • Sam (normal): What? No, I don't hear anything.
  • Quinn (happy): Exactly...it's so quiet. It's so peaceful…
  • Sam (surprised): You think that's all the drama over for a while?
  • Quinn (awkward): Maybe…it'd be nice to take a break.
  • Sam (happy): A break? No, no. There's no time for a break!
  • Quinn (happy): Right. We've still got a reputation to build.
  • Sam (normal): And I've been thinking about how to do it...
  • Quinn (confident): Oh yeah? Is this another big-brain Sam plan? Let's hear it.
  • Sam (happy): We should host a cook-off – and invite the whole town!
  • Quinn (awkward): What? You mean like…we should host a COMPETITION?
  • Sam (surprised): Exactly! We need a good gimmick.
  • Sam (normal): Lori's got her fancy chef, Buoy Boy's got the 'pub' thing.
  • Quinn (confident): Okay, you've convinced me. A cook-off! Let's do it.
